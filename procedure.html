📘 ADVERBIALS OF SEQUENCE

GRAMMAR DATABASE // PROCEDURE TEXT
  • FIRST / FIRSTLY — membuka rangkaian langkah.
  • NEXT / THEN / SUBSEQUENTLY / AFTER THAT — melanjutkan urutan langkah.
  • FINALLY / LASTLY — menutup rangkaian langkah.

CARA PAKAI

Biasanya diletakkan di awal kalimat dan diikuti koma, lalu kalimat imperative (Verb 1 tanpa subjek):

First, identify the problem. Next, prepare the tools. Finally, verify the result.

Struktur Procedure Text: Goal/Aim → Steps → (Result). Ciri bahasa: imperative verbs (initialize, verify, encrypt, override, bypass…), simple present, adverbials of sequence.
