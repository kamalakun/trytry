📘 CONJUNCTION DATABASE

EFFECT ← CAUSE

because + clause (S+V): "Coastal cities flood because sea levels rise."

because of / due to / owing to + noun phrase: "The delay was due to heavy rain." (due to sering mengikuti be/noun.)

stems from / is caused by: efek disebut lebih dulu, lalu penyebabnya.

CAUSE → EFFECT

leads to / results in / triggers + noun phrase: "Deforestation leads to severe flooding."

💡 PRO TIP

Lihat apa yang mengikuti konektor: jika clause (ada subjek + kata kerja) → pakai because; jika noun phrase → pakai because of / due to. Lalu cek arahnya: Cause→Effect atau Effect←Cause.
